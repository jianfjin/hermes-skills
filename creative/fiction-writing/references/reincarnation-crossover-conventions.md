# Reincarnation Crossover Conventions (转世系列)

## Premise

A character from the cultivation world reincarnates into the modern world with no memory of their past life. Peak Ge identifies them via 灵力探识海 (spiritual energy probing), partially unlocks the memory seal, and applies the standard 师母 process.

## Identification Method

1. Peak Ge touches the character's forehead and sends spiritual energy into their 识海 (sea of consciousness)
2. Inside the 识海, he sees the original xianxia character's 元神 (primordial spirit) in chains/seal — the 转世轮回 memory seal
3. The seal is different from 封神丹 (which Peak Ge used in the cultivation world) — it's a natural轮回封印
4. A system confirmation dialog appears: 「叮！方清雪转世体确认：李小冉。道体匹配度：97%。记忆封印状态：完整。当前元阴状态：完整（处子）。建议：按标准流程全面开发。」
5. Peak Ge partially unlocks the seal — enough to create soul-level resonance and confusion, but NOT full memory recovery

## Character Design

The reincarnated character has:
- **Modern identity**: A modern person with a modern career (e.g., 娱乐圈明星李小冉)
- **Maintained xianxia features**: Same 道体 constitution, 处子元阴 intact, same mannerisms (微蹙眉时眉宇间的弧度、端起酒杯时指尖的姿势、与人交谈时下巴微抬的角度)
- **Aging carried over**: Can be 中年美妇/熟女 (late 30s-40s) rather than young
- **Body parameters carried over**: Same breast/waist/hip measurements as xianxia version (or slightly evolved, e.g., 34D → 36D with age)

## Narrative Voice

- Use the **modern name** (李小冉) for all narrative text
- Use the **xianxia name** (方清雪) only in:
  - Peak Ge's internal thoughts
  - 识海/probing scenes
  - Narration of past-life memories
- This maintains the dual identity without confusing the reader

## Jewelry and Elegance Contrast

When the character enters the scene:
- Describe high-end brand clothing (知名品牌高定紧身裙)
- Describe jewelry explicitly (钻石项链、钻石耳环、名贵腕表)
- Describe the 高贵/堕落反差 throughout the scene — the jewelry on her naked body, the expensive dress crumpled beside the table, etc.

## Series Placement

The reincarnation crossover bridges the xianxia and modern sub-universes:
- NO other modern characters (师母, 蒋洁) appear in this series
- NO cultivation world characters (陆凡, 玄冥老祖) appear in this series
- The crossover is ONLY through the reincarnated character's 前世 identity
- The narrative action takes place in the modern setting (KTV包厢, 寓所, 餐桌, etc.)
