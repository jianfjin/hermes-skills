## When to Use

- The user wants a scene that combines TECHNICAL OUTPUT with erotic content (not just sex with tech window dressing)

## Common Elements Across All Three

1. **Silent penetrating partner.** 赵小龙 never speaks. All communication is through the laptop screen (typed markdown lists, code, TODOs). His silence is his power — he can write code, do architecture, and provide feedback all without interrupting his rhythm.

2. **Real output.** The code, TODO, and review must be genuine. A future user should be able to use the TODO to implement features, or fix bugs from the review findings. No props.

3. **The character's professional voice is amplified, not muted.** 李小冉 counts thrust frequencies, 飞飞 uses self-attention metaphors, 黛米 grades everything on a startup scale. The sex doesn't dilute their voice — it forces it into a purer form because they're under cognitive load.

4. **The laptop screen as a second point of penetration.** The character reads feedback/code/TODOs while being fucked. The screen is a second focus point that divides their attention, making the penetration feel more invasive. When they choose the SCREEN over the partner's rhythm, it's a statement.

5. **Score at the end.** Every variant ends with a quantified score or verdict (code quality grade, TODO completeness score, review verdict). This gives the work output narrative closure.

## When NOT to Use

- The output would be embarrassing if read without context (code quality must be passable)
- The working character doesn't have a clear professional voice (needs distinct perspective)
- The scene is purely about the sex — if the work output is a prop, this pattern backfires
- The penetrating character would speak (breaks 赵小龙's signature silence)
