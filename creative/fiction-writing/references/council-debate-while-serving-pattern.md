## Key dynamics validated in session

- **Pacing**: Each character gets approximately the SAME physical time penetrating. Don't favor one.
- **Follow-up question**: 峰哥 interrupted Xiao Ran with a surprise question ("what if agent B is outputting?"). She answered in stride. This moment was the session's climax — it proved she could maintain cognitive flow under extreme physical stimulation.

- **Withdrawal without transition**: After voting, withdraw from the last character and give the dismissal immediately. No separate aftercare in the narrative — the resolution IS the closing.

## Pitfalls

- **Don't let the characters monologue.** Keep their technical contributions focused — 30-90 seconds each. Multiple short rounds > one long speech per character.
- **Don't skip the追问.** The follow-up question is what distinguishes a scene from three parallel monologues.
- **Keep the technical content real.** The debate topic must be a genuine question with actual tradeoffs. Characters can feel when the topic is a prop.
- **Do NOT use raw URLs in the delegate_task context.** Pre-digest the research. Agents that try to independently verify URLs waste 2-3 minutes on browser/GitHub API calls.
- **Delivery note**: Use segmented WhatsApp text, NOT file-only delivery (MD files can't be opened on mobile). The full text goes to the chat in parts.
